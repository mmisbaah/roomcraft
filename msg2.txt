Give library items real artwork instead of flat colour swatches

Item cards (and the detail panel preview) showed a single flat rectangle of
item.color, so all 460 pieces looked identical apart from a label.

Adds ItemThumb, which draws each piece as inline SVG from its category and
kind, tinted with its own palette (color + accent, with darker/lighter
derivatives for depth) and scaled by its real w x d, so a 2.9 m sectional
reads differently from a 0.38 m bar stool. Nothing is downloaded: the artwork
is vector, so the whole set costs a few KB of JS rather than 460 bitmaps,
and it stays sharp at any card size.

Category is resolved before kind because several kinds are ambiguous across
categories - `panel` is both a wall panel and a panelled bed head, `murphy`
is both a storage wall-unit and a bed - so "Cloud Panel Bed" and "Hide Murphy
Bed" would otherwise render as cabinets.

Locked cards desaturate and dim instead of fading out, so the piece stays
recognisable behind the padlock.
